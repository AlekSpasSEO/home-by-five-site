Automation and cost savings

Find the repetitive work. Cost it. Remove it.

This is a separate service from marketing, and a genuinely different kind of value. Marketing grows the top line. This one takes cost out of the middle, and the saving is measurable in a way marketing rarely is.

How it is priced

Against measured savings, not against hours.

Marketing automations needed to deliver the marketing service are included in your package. Large custom operational builds are scoped and priced separately, because they are a different piece of work.

Where it fits, we may charge an agreed share of measured first-year cost savings, currently set at 20%. That figure is a configured default, agreed per engagement, and calculated from the measured result rather than the projection.

If the baseline shows the saving is small, we will tell you the build is not worth doing. That happens, and it is a better outcome than an automation nobody uses.

The sequence

Measure first. Always.

  1. 01

    Baseline what it costs today

    Before anything gets built, we measure what the current process actually costs in hours, salary and lost revenue. If we cannot measure it, we do not claim a saving on it.

    • Process mapping
    • Time and cost per run
    • Failure and leakage points
  2. 02

    Calculate the measurable saving

    A number you can check, built from the baseline. This is the part most automation pitches skip, which is why most automation pitches are unfalsifiable.

    • Projected hours removed
    • Projected revenue recovered
    • Confidence and assumptions stated
  3. 03

    Agree scope, then agree price

    In that order. Scope first so the price is anchored to something real, rather than the price coming first and the scope bending to fit it.

    • Written scope
    • Acceptance criteria
    • What is explicitly out
  4. 04

    Build, measure, hand over

    You own what gets built. We measure against the baseline afterwards, and where the model includes a share of savings, that share is calculated from the measured result, not the projection.

    • Build and test
    • Post-implementation measurement
    • Documentation and handover

Why we do this at all

Because the point of the company is to give the owner their evening back.

Growth that adds administrative load is not really growth. It is the same business with more paperwork.
