## Selection notes

Chosen against the brand rules: documentary and candid rather than posed studio work,
diverse in age, ethnicity and setting, ordinary homes rather than luxury interiors, and
no single-culture nuclear-family default.

Two categories were deliberately rejected:

- **Vans and shopfronts carrying a real company's livery.** One strong service-van photo
  showed a named plumbing firm. On a marketing site that reads as a client or partner
  claim, so it was dropped.
- **"Team around a laptop" office stock.** Every result was the exact cliche the brand
  rules ban. The About page uses a family image instead of a fake team photo.

## Replacing an image

Keep the filename, drop in the new file, and update the row above plus the `alt` text in
`src/config/photography.ts`. Nothing else references the images directly.
